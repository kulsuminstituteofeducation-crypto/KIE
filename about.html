About Kulsum Institute of Education

Dedicated to spreading authentic Islamic knowledge through structured online education for students across the world.

Who We Are

An Online Home for Learning the Qur’an

Kulsum Institute of Education is an online Islamic learning platform committed to providing quality Quran and Islamic education for children, adults, brothers and sisters worldwide.

Our objective is to make authentic Islamic knowledge accessible from anywhere while maintaining proper Islamic etiquette, discipline and qualified teaching methods.

Students receive personalized guidance from experienced instructors through flexible online classes suitable for every age group.

Our Mission

Learning Correctly, Living It Daily

To help Muslims strengthen their relationship with Allah by learning the Quran correctly, understanding Islamic teachings and implementing them in daily life.
